Bibliography & Resources

Manuscripts, scholarship and reference works for reading Italian account books, placing them in context, and working with them through HTR.

  • Medici, Alamanno de’. Ledger of Medici Accounts. 1465–1494. Oversize Ms. Codex 1321. Kislak Center for Special Collections, Rare Books and Manuscripts, University of Pennsylvania Libraries, Philadelphia, PA. https://colenda.library.upenn.edu/catalog/81431-p35q4rn2w.
  • Medici, Alamanno de’. “Ledger of Medici Debtors and Creditors and Accounts.” 1475–1483. Ms. Codex 1508. Kislak Center for Special Collections, Rare Books and Manuscripts, University of Pennsylvania Libraries, Philadelphia, PA. https://colenda.library.upenn.edu/catalog/81431-p3wh2dg26.
  • “Ledger of Medici Accounts and Memoranda.” 1456–1495. Oversize Ms. Codex 1435. Kislak Center for Special Collections, Rare Books and Manuscripts, University of Pennsylvania Libraries, Philadelphia, PA. https://colenda.library.upenn.edu/catalog/81431-p3m96z.

Account books: genre, practice and editions

  • Arlinghaus, F.-J. “Account Books.” In Transforming the Medieval World: Uses of Literacy in the Middle Ages, edited by F.-J. Arlinghaus, M. Ostermann, O. Plessow, and G. Tscherpeln, 43–70. Turnhout: Brepols, 2006.
  • De Roover, Raymond. “The Development of Accounting Prior to Luca Pacioli According to the Account-books of Medieval Merchants.” In Studies in the History of Accounting, edited by A. C. Littleton and B. S. Yamey, 114–74. Homewood, IL: Richard D. Irwin, 1956.
  • Goldthwaite, Richard. “The Practice and Culture of Accounting in Renaissance Florence.” Enterprise & Society 16, no. 3 (2015): 611–47. https://doi.org/10.1017/eso.2015.17.
  • Rucellai, Giunta di Nardo. Il Libro discepoli e pigione del tintore Giunta di Nardo Rucellai: (Firenze, 1341–46). Edited by Mathieu Harsch. Pisa: Edizioni della Normale, 2018.
  • Sangster, Alan, Greg Stoner, Paul De Lange, Brendan O’Connell, and Giovanna Scataglini-Belghitar. “Pacioli’s Forgotten Book: The Merchant’s ‘Ricordanze.’” The Accounting Historians Journal 39, no. 2 (2012): 27–44. https://doi.org/10.2308/0148-4184.39.2.27.
  • Tarallo, Giovanni. Il Quaternus del tesoriere di Lecce Giovanni Tarallo: 1473–1474. Edited by Benedetto Vetere. Rome: Istituto storico italiano per il Medio Evo, 2018.

  • Vine, Angus Edmund. Early Modern Merchants and Their Books. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2025.
