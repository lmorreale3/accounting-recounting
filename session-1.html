Overview

The inaugural workshop introduces participants to the merchant ledger as a rich historical and literary genre. We explore how structural design, entry formulas, and bookkeeping habits reflect both practical financial management and underlying social networks.

  • Understanding the Genre. Introduction to the account book as a genre. How are they organized, what varieties of documents co-exist within them, and how do form and message work together?
  • Introduction to MS 1321. Orientation to UPenn MS 1321, one of Alamanno di Medici's account books. Participants learn common words and phrases, syntactic construction of records, and techniques for identifying protagonists and characters within the ledger.
  • Collaborative Transcription Workflows. How to use FromThePage for collaborative transcription with eScriptorium as the potential downstream audience. Discussion of benefits, drawbacks, and practical strategies for successful group transcription.
  • Paleographical & Lexical Resources. Survey of essential tools and reference works for reading Italian mercantesca (and other manuscript hands), alongside specialized resources for Italian business history and terminology.
