Overview

Guided by Dr. Elena Brizio (Georgetown University, Villa Le Balze), participants will handle the physical manuscript to contrast material engagement with screen-based analysis.

  • Hands-on Manuscript Examination. Direct physical engagement with MS 1321, led by Dr. Elena Brizio. Investigating whether physical interaction alters our understanding and interpretation of the accounts.
