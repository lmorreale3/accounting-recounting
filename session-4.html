Overview

The final workshop evaluates model transferability across distinct manuscript volumes and transforms transcribed primary source text into structured dataset visualizations.

  • Model Testing on MS 1321. Applying our refined and corrected HTR model against full sample pages of UPenn MS 1321 to evaluate line and word error rates.
  • Cross-Manuscript Application (MS 1508). Testing the trained model on MS 1508, a second account book written by Alamanno di Medici held at Penn Libraries, to evaluate hand recognition across distinct volumes.
  • Data Extraction & Visualization. Extracting structured data from both account books and generating comparative visual datasets.
  • Methodological Synthesis. Reflecting on how to effectively balance granular textual anecdote with macro-level data analytics in historical research.
  • Final Demonstration: Leggio and Conti. A closing demonstration of how the capacities of the tools used throughout the series can be brought together in two purpose-built applications: Leggio for transcription, HTR and LLM-assisted reading, and Conti for data exploration and visualization. Demonstration only; participants do not need to install anything. See the Workspace page for more.
