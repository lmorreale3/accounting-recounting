Overview

The second session moves from initial transcription challenges to contextualizing Alamanno's accounts within broader archival landscapes and initiating Handwritten Text Recognition (HTR) workflows.

  • Troubleshooting Transcription. Addressing transcription difficulties, paleographical roadblocks, and syntax ambiguities encountered during initial participant transcription assignments.
  • Archival & Historical Context. Placing Alamanno's account book in the context of the many extant late medieval and early modern Florentine sources.
  • Introduction to eScriptorium. Demonstrating eScriptorium as an HTR platform, with dedicated focus on automatic and manual page segmentation techniques.
  • HTR Goals & Potential. Explaining the objective of building a working Handwritten Text Recognition model and evaluating the transformative potential of HTR for the financial accounts genre.

Perform layout analysis and segment your assigned pages within the Penn eScriptorium interface.
